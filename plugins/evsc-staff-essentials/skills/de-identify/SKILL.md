# De-identify

Help EVSC staff remove information that could identify a student, family member
or staff member before they share something. This is a helper the staff member
chooses to use. It doesn't replace their judgment, EVSC policy or the district's
data-sharing approval process, and the staff member is responsible for what
they share.

## The standard

FERPA treats information as identifying when it would let "a reasonable person
in the school community, who does not have personal knowledge of the relevant
circumstances," identify a student "with reasonable certainty," alone or in
combination with other information (34 CFR 99.3). Records can be shared without
consent only after all such information is removed and a reasonable
determination is made that the student can't be identified, "whether through
single or multiple releases, and taking into account other reasonably available
information" (34 CFR 99.31(b)(1)).

So removing names is not enough. Ask: could someone at this school, or in this
community, work out who this is?

## What to remove or change

**Direct identifiers: always remove**

- Names of students, parents, guardians, siblings and other family members
- Student ID numbers, state test IDs, Social Security numbers, account or lunch numbers
- Home addresses, phone numbers, personal email addresses, usernames
- Photos, faces, signatures, handwriting samples, voice or video
- Dates of birth and places of birth
- Staff names and IDs when the content is about that person's performance,
  evaluation, discipline, health or personnel matters

**Indirect identifiers: generalize, or remove if they still point to one person**

- Exact dates (birthdays, incidents, meetings, enrollment) → month and year, or "this fall"
- School name together with grade, class, team, bus route or teacher → drop or widen
  ("a middle school", "a 7th grader")
- Rare characteristics: a specific disability or diagnosis, IEP or 504 status, English
  learner status, homelessness, foster care, immigration status, pregnancy, discipline,
  free or reduced lunch, a unique award or event
- Ages for very young or unusually old students → an age range
- Teacher names in a class-level example (they narrow it to one classroom)
- Small groups: in counts, tables or charts, don't show groups small enough that
  people could be recognized. Common practice is to suppress groups under 10; follow
  EVSC's rule if it differs, and check that totals can't be used to work out a hidden
  number.

**Places identifiers hide in files**

- File names, document titles, headers and footers
- Comments, tracked changes, speaker notes, alt text
- Hidden rows, columns and sheets, filters, formulas that pull from other files,
  pivot-table caches
- Document properties (author, last modified by)
- Text inside images, screenshots and scanned pages, including browser tabs and
  notifications in screenshots
- Email threads quoted below the latest message, and email signatures

Point out the places you can't fully check (for example, document properties of
a file you were only given as text) so the staff member can check them.

## How to de-identify

1. **Work only with what's in the conversation.** Don't search the web, fetch pages
   or use connectors with the content while de-identifying it.
2. **Replace consistently.** Use the same placeholder for the same person every time
   (`Student A`, `Student B`, `Parent of Student A`, `Teacher 1`, `[School]`,
   `[Date]`), so the text still makes sense.
3. **Generalize before you delete** when the detail matters to the purpose
   (a grade band instead of a grade, a month instead of a date). Delete when it
   doesn't.
4. **Keep the meaning.** Don't change scores, wording or facts the staff member
   needs, other than the identifiers.
5. **Files:** when given a document or spreadsheet, produce a new de-identified
   copy with a neutral file name. Never overwrite the original.

## What to return

1. The de-identified version, ready to use.
2. A short summary of what changed, by type ("6 student names replaced, 2 dates
   generalized to the month, 1 school name removed"). Don't repeat the removed
   values in the summary.
3. **Remaining concerns:** anything you weren't sure about or couldn't check,
   such as a detail that might still identify someone in the school community,
   small groups, or file properties and images.

Only provide a key that maps placeholders back to real names if the staff member
asks for one. Give it separately, and remind them to keep it apart from the
de-identified version and never share the two together.

## When to stop and refer

- **Public release, research, or data going to a vendor or outside agency:**
  de-identifying it here is a first step only. Tell the staff member to follow
  EVSC's data-sharing approval process.
- **Records requests, legal matters, investigations or subpoenas:** don't prepare
  redacted records for these. Refer the staff member to their administrator or
  the appropriate EVSC office.
- **Very small or unique situations** (one student in a program, a widely known
  incident): say plainly that the content may not be de-identifiable while still
  being useful, and suggest sharing a general description instead.
